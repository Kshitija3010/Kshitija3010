# प्राचीन रसोई · Ep 1 · दुनिया की पहली रोटी — Treatment

Channel: **Kathaverse** · Style: Tablet Sketch Explainer (`ipad-sketch`) · Hindi · ~90 s film, delivered as
5 s Kathaverse intro + 1.5 s thumbnail card + film, in 16:9 (1920×1080) and 9:16 (1080×1920).

## 1. Three structures
1. **A single journey (grain → bread).** Follow one grain of wild wheat from the plant, through the grinding stone, dough and fire, to a flatbread.
2. **A detective story, told backwards.** Open on burnt crumbs in a fireplace, then work back to who made them and how.
3. **A myth, then the correction.** "First farming, then bread" is written on the page, then struck out and rewritten as "first bread, then farming?".

**Chosen: 3 framing 1.** The page opens with a question; the "farming → bread" arrow is drawn and later **reversed**, which is the turn. The middle is the journey (structure 1), because a process is what this style is best at. The discovery beat (structure 2) proves the claim.

## 2. Logline and arc
14,400 years ago, hunter-gatherers in Jordan's Black Desert baked flatbread, at least 4,000 years before farming began.
Setup: everyone assumes farming came first → the journey of the first bread → turn: the arrow flips, so bread came first → ending: "would you eat it?", plus the follow card.

## 3. Benchmark
Time-lapse recordings of illustrators sketching recipes on a tablet, plus short food-history explainers.
Learn: one page that fills up, slow camera drift, colour as information (the dough browning).
Don't take: anyone's drawings, lettering or layouts.

## 4. Page plan (one canvas, 3600×2400 units)
Top left: the question title. Top right: a small map of the Levant with a coral dot (Shubayqa).
Middle row, the process: grain stalk + tuber → grinding stone → dough ball → flatbread on hot stones (fire).
Bottom left: fireplace and charred crumbs with a magnifier circle. Bottom right: timeline (bread 14,400 → farming ~10,000+ → today).
Space for the final line ("पहले रोटी, फिर खेती?") stays empty until the turn.

## 6. Cue map
~96 BPM, 4/4. Nylon guitar + soft bass + shaker + kalimba accent on each new idea. Near-silence before the reversal (shot 8); the reversal lands with a kalimba + bass note.

## 7. Sound design
Pencil foley driven by the marks (line / letters / colouring), sheet slide on gestures, a crackling fire bed under shots 4–6 (low), and a card hit on the thumbnail card.

## 8. Titles and subtitles
Page lettering: Kalam (OFL, Devanagari + Latin), pencilled through the grain threshold. Subtitles: a paper chip at the bottom left, Hindi in Poppins Devanagari, one or two lines.
