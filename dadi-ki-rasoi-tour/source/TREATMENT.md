# दादी की रसोई का टूर · Treatment

Channel: **Kathaverse** · Style: 1930s Rubber Hose Cartoon (`rubber-hose`) · Hindi · ~90 s film, delivered as
5 s Kathaverse intro + 1.5 s thumbnail card + film, in 16:9 (4:3 gate) and 9:16 (3:4 gate).
Grey ramp: a faint **sepia print** (warm), kept for the whole film; the Kathaverse logo is shown toned to the same sepia.

## 1. Three structures
1. **A talent show.** Each tool takes the stage in turn; the smallest one wins.
2. **A singalong.** A bouncing ball over the tool names while the set acts out each line.
3. **A guided tour (chosen).** A host walks along one long kitchen counter; at each station a tool wakes up and performs its job to the band, Mickey-moused. The user asked for a *tour*, and a trucking pan along one set is the style's own "tabletop pan".

## 2. Logline and arc
Lotu-ram, a brass लोटा with rubber-hose arms, white gloves and big shoes, guides us through grandmother's kitchen of a hundred years ago, where every tool is alive.
Setup: the iris opens on Lotu, who bows → seven stations, each a gag where the tool does its real job (fire, grinding, pounding, milling, churning, winnowing, cooling) → finale: the whole kitchen dances "बिना बिजली, बिना मशीन" → Lotu pulls the Kathaverse end card over the scene.

## 3. Cast (original designs: the whole object is the body)
- **लोटूराम (Lotu)**: a round brass lota (body = the pot, a short neck, a flared rim like a hat brim), pie eyes, wide grin, rubber-hose arms and legs, four-finger gloves, big shoes. The secondary feature is a tuft of steam rising from the mouth of the pot.
- **The tools, each with pie eyes and a mouth on its body; they breathe on the beat:**
  - चूल्हा (clay stove, with a फुंकनी blow-pipe)
  - सिल-बट्टा (flat grinding stone and its roller stone)
  - ओखली-मूसल (mortar and long wooden pestle)
  - चक्की (two-stone hand mill with a peg handle)
  - मथानी (wooden churn stick in a pot of curd, turned with a rope)
  - सूप (winnowing basket)
  - मटका (clay water pot)

## 4. Shot list (beats at 120 bpm; 1 beat = 12 frames)
| # | Content | Camera | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Iris opens on Lotu's eye, then widens; Lotu bows | iris + locked medium | opening: an iris on one eye |
| 2 | Title card "दादी की रसोई" with hopping letters | locked | the film is an object |
| 3–9 | Seven stations along one counter: Lotu walks in (a step per beat), the tool wakes, the routine, a stinger | trucking pan between stations, locked medium at each | one prop per gag, faces readable |
| 10 | Finale: pull-back, everything dances, the background squashes on the floor line | pull-back to a packed medium-full | one feeling spreads |
| 11 | Lotu drags the Kathaverse card over the scene (end card: logo, comment, follow) | locked | ending: the card pulled over |

Routines (Mickey-mousing):
- **चूल्हा**: Lotu blows the फुंकनी; it sounds like a trumpet; the flame grows on each note.
- **सिल-बट्टा**: the batta rolls back and forth on the beat, washboard scrape; green chutney appears.
- **ओखली-मूसल**: the pestle pounds on 1 and 3 like a bass drum; husk flies off the grain.
- **चक्की**: Lotu turns the handle; the stone spins like a record; flour pours out; the चक्की gets dizzy.
- **मथानी**: the rope pull is a trombone smear; butter pops up on top.
- **सूप**: tossing grain on the beat; chaff flies away (slide whistle); grain stays.
- **मटका**: it "sweats" droplets that evaporate; a cool breeze (wind whistle); it shivers happily.

## 5. Sound
1930s hot band at 120 bpm: stride piano, tuba oom-pah, banjo on 2 & 4, brushed snare, clarinet and trumpet; an original syncopated theme. A stinger for each gag; band stops before two gags. Foley by material (clay, stone, wood, brass). The voice is a Hindi vaudeville MC through a radio chain, followed by an optical-sound ageing pass.

## 6. Titles and subtitles
Title in a fat Devanagari display face (white face, ink outline, drop shadow, hopping letters). Subtitles go on period title cards (near-black plate, white double rule) with a Devanagari text face.
Channel kit: the Kathaverse corner logo in sepia, outside the gate, from 2 s; the end card is a title card with the logo, "कमेंट करें" and "फॉलो करें · Kathaverse".
