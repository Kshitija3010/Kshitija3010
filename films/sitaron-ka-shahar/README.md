# सितारों का शहर · City of Stars

An original flash-cut lyric Short for Astroverse (9:16, 48 s, 120 BPM). The music, lyrics and visuals are all made in code, so the video is yours to post with no copyright claims.

Structure: intro "सुनो… आसमान कुछ कहता है…" → verse "रात के आसमान में छुपा है एक शहर · बारह दरवाज़े, बारह राज़ · हर तारा एक कहानी, हर कहानी तुम्हारी" → build "कौन-सा दरवाज़ा तुम्हारा है? 3-2-1" → drop with all 12 rashis, one per second → title "सितारों का शहर" → outro "तुम्हारी राशि कौन-सी? कमेंट करो ↓ फॉलो करो" → Astroverse logo.

Music: kick, claps and hats synthesised in code; a sitar-like plucked hook (shamisen model); sub bass; VCSL frame drums, gong and hand chimes (CC0).
